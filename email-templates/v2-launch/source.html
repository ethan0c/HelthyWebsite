We rebuilt Helthy.
Here's what's new.

v2 is live on iOS and Android. This is the biggest update since launch: smarter AI, deeper insights, and a lot of things that should've been there from day one.

01

AI got a lot smarter.

Body fat estimates from progress photos. Plateau detection with a fix plan. Goal ETA predictor. Weekly physique report. Strength progression forecast. Your coach now sees four weeks ahead.

02

Insights that actually mean something.

Momentum Ring, signal cards, and a weekly narrative written in plain English. Your coach now summarises your whole week — what worked, what didn't, and what to do next.

03

Logging got faster.

Nutrition label scanner, quick meal entry, photo logging, and food suggestions for common meals. Swipe to delete. Import your history from MFP, MacroFactor, Hevy, Strong, and more.

04

Android is here + 6 languages.

Helthy is now on Google Play. Full support for Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, and Chinese. Share the app with anyone, anywhere.
